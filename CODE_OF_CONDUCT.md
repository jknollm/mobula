# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on technical substance.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after warning.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces and in public spaces when representing this project.

## Reporting

Report concerns to project maintainers through the repository issue tracker or direct maintainer contact channels.
